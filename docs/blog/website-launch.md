# Introducing the New Freeware Advanced Audio Website

*Published: September 2026*

Welcome to the new official website for the **Freeware Advanced Audio** organization!

We are excited to announce a complete refresh of our online presence, documentation, and web demonstration tools for **FAAC** and **FAAD**.

---

## What's New?

### 1. Migrated Repositories & Clean Organization

We have officially moved our source repositories from personal accounts (`knik0/faac` and `knik0/faad2`) to the official GitHub organization: <i class="fa-solid fa-arrow-right"></i> **[github.com/FreewareAdvancedAudio](https://github.com/FreewareAdvancedAudio)**

### 2. The FAAC 2.0+ LGPL Era

If you remember FAAC from over a decade ago, you might recall historical discussions around licensing or audio quality. With **FAAC 2.0+**, the encoder was developed under **LGPL v2.1+**:

- **Clean ISO-Free Codebase**: No legacy ISO reference code.
- **LGPL v2.1+ Free Software**: FOSS-friendly licensing suitable for inclusion in open-source and commercial products alike.
- **SBR & HE-AAC Support**: Spectral Band Replication for low-bitrate encoding.
- **Efficient Encoding**: C libraries for batch conversion, media applications, and embedded workflows.

### 3. Interactive In-Browser Encoder

You can now test FAAC AAC encoding **directly inside your web browser** on our new [browser converter](/playground). No installation required!

### 4. Comprehensive Documentation

Explore our updated CLI guides and C API documentation:

- [FAAC Encoder Documentation](/docs/faac)
- [FAAD Decoder Documentation](/docs/faad)

## A fresh listen for longtime FAAC users

If older FAAC releases disappointed you, we welcome your scrutiny of the current encoder. Try your own music, follow our [listening guide](/docs/listening), and share reproducible findings. Your listening results help guide further improvements.

## Future Roadmap

We are actively working on continuous quality tuning and optimizations for FAAC, as well as the rollout of **FAAD3**—a C11 rewrite under **LGPL v2.1+** bringing LGPL licensing and performance enhancements to the decoder library alongside FAAD2 (GPL v2+, C99).

Stay tuned for more updates!
