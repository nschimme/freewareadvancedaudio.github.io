# Introducing the New Freeware Advanced Audio Website

*Published: September 2026*

Welcome to the new official website for the **Freeware Advanced Audio** organization!

We are excited to announce a complete refresh of our online presence, documentation, and web demonstration tools for **FAAC** and **FAAD2**.

---

## What's New?

### 1. Migrated Repositories & Clean Organization

We have officially moved our source repositories from personal accounts (`knik0/faac` and `knik0/faad2`) to the official GitHub organization: 👉 **[github.com/FreewareAdvancedAudio](https://github.com/FreewareAdvancedAudio)**

### 2. The FAAC 2.0+ LGPL Era

If you remember FAAC from over a decade ago, you might recall historical discussions around licensing or audio quality. With **FAAC 2.0+**, the encoder was completely **rewritten from scratch under LGPL v2.1+**:

- **Clean ISO-Free Codebase**: No legacy ISO reference code.
- **LGPL v2.1+ Free Software**: FOSS-friendly licensing suitable for inclusion in open-source and commercial products alike.
- **SBR & HE-AAC Support**: Spectral Band Replication for low-bitrate encoding.
- **Superior Throughput**: Outstanding encoding speed with 3-5x performance headroom.

### 3. Interactive In-Browser Converter

You can now test FAAC AAC encoding and FAAD2 decoding **directly inside your web browser** on our new [Interactive Playground](/playground) or right on the [Homepage](/). No installation required!

### 4. Comprehensive Documentation

Explore our updated CLI guides and C API documentation:

- [FAAC Encoder Documentation](/docs/faac)
- [FAAD2 Decoder Documentation](/docs/faad2)
- [Codec Comparison & Licensing Clearhouse](/docs/comparison)
